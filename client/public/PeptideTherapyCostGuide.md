# How Much Does Peptide Therapy Cost in 2026? A Complete Pricing Guide

One of the most common questions patients ask when considering regenerative medicine is: *"How much does peptide therapy actually cost?"* Because peptide therapy is highly personalized and rarely covered by traditional insurance, pricing can vary wildly depending on the clinic, the specific peptide, and the compounding pharmacy used.

In this comprehensive 2026 guide, we break down the average costs of the most popular peptide therapies, explain what factors influence the price, and help you understand what you should expect to pay for a legitimate, medically supervised protocol.

## Average Monthly Costs by Peptide Category

Peptide costs are typically broken down into a monthly supply. Below are the national averages for pharmaceutical-grade peptides prescribed by a licensed practitioner and sourced from a 503A or 503B compounding pharmacy.

| Peptide Category | Common Peptides | Average Monthly Cost |
|-----------------|-----------------|---------------------|
| Weight Loss / GLP-1s | Semaglutide, Tirzepatide | $250 - $600 / month |
| Healing & Recovery | BPC-157, TB-500 | $150 - $350 / month |
| Growth Hormone | CJC-1295, Ipamorelin | $200 - $450 / month |
| Sexual Health | PT-141, Kisspeptin | $100 - $300 / month |

| Cognitive Enhancement | Semax, Selank | $100 - $250 / month |
| Fat Loss (non-GLP-1) | AOD-9604, 5-Amino-1MQ | $150 - $350 / month |

## What's Included in the Cost?

Most peptide therapy programs include several components beyond just the peptide itself:

### Initial Consultation ($150 - $500)
A comprehensive evaluation including health history review, lab work interpretation, and protocol design. Many clinics offer this as a one-time fee that covers your first protocol.

### Lab Work ($200 - $800)
Baseline bloodwork is essential for safe peptide therapy. This typically includes a comprehensive metabolic panel, hormone levels, inflammatory markers, and sometimes genetic testing.

### The Peptide Itself ($100 - $500/month)
The actual cost of the compounded peptide varies based on the specific peptide, dosage, and pharmacy. 503B pharmacies (FDA-registered outsourcing facilities) tend to be slightly more expensive but offer additional quality assurance.

### Follow-Up Visits ($75 - $200 each)
Most protocols require monthly or quarterly check-ins to monitor progress, adjust dosing, and review lab results.

### Supplies ($20 - $50/month)
Syringes, alcohol swabs, bacteriostatic water, and sharps containers are usually an additional cost.

## Factors That Influence Price

Several factors can significantly affect what you'll pay for peptide therapy:

**Geographic Location:** Clinics in major metropolitan areas (NYC, LA, Miami) typically charge 20-40% more than those in smaller markets.

**Practitioner Credentials:** Board-certified physicians and specialized anti-aging practitioners may charge more than nurse practitioners or PAs, though quality of care varies by individual.

**Pharmacy Source:** 503B pharmacies are generally more expensive than 503A but offer batch testing and higher regulatory oversight. Brand-name GLP-1s (Ozempic, Mounjaro) are significantly more expensive than compounded versions.

**Protocol Complexity:** Single-peptide protocols cost less than multi-peptide stacks. Combination therapies (e.g., CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin + BPC-157) will naturally cost more.

**Duration:** Many clinics offer discounted rates for 3-month or 6-month commitments versus month-to-month pricing.

## Insurance Coverage

As of 2026, most peptide therapies are **not covered by traditional health insurance**, with a few exceptions:

- **Semaglutide** (as Ozempic/Wegovy) may be covered for diabetes or obesity with prior authorization
- **Tirzepatide** (as Mounjaro/Zepbound) may be covered for Type 2 diabetes
- **Testosterone** is often covered when prescribed for diagnosed hypogonadism

Most other peptides (BPC-157, CJC-1295, Ipamorelin, PT-141, etc.) are considered "off-label" or "experimental" and are paid out-of-pocket. Some clinics accept HSA/FSA funds.

## Red Flags: When the Price is Too Low

Be cautious of peptide therapy that seems unusually cheap. Warning signs include:

- Peptides priced under $50/month (may indicate research-grade, not pharmaceutical-grade)
- No required lab work or consultation
- No prescription required
- Sourced from overseas or unregulated suppliers
- No follow-up monitoring included
